About Us

AMP Automotive Services is locally owned and family-operated since 2003.

We have been voted the top performing automotive service center in the tri-county region for the 5th year in a row by the JD Power annual survey. All of our mechanics are ASE certfied and are recertified every 4 years. We provide the highest level of service to every customer that walks through our door to build and maintain a trusting relationship to make our customers become family.

We invite you in to see for yourself and to have a free cup of coffee!
