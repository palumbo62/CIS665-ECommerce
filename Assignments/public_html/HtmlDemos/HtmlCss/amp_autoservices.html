AMP Automotive Services

Welcome!

Thank you for taking the time to visit our website. AMP Automotive Services is a full-service auto repair and maintenance center which has been in operation since 2003. We will service any domestic or imported vehicle offering the highest level of quality service and customer satisfaction.

From brake service and repair to oil changes and fluid top-offs we have you covered. We have four service centers conveniently located in the metro area all providing the same great service. If you need assistance getting your vehicle to any service center, we provide towing services free of charge when we perform the repairs.

We are independently owned and operated and take great pride in getting the job done right the first time every time.
